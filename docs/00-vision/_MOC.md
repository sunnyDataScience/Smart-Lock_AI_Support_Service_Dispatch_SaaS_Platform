# Vision Zone -- Why We Build

This zone contains the foundational "why" documents: the product vision, business case, user journeys, and competitive strategy. Everything here answers the question **"What problem are we solving and for whom?"**

---

## Relationship to Other Zones

- **Downstream:** [[01-design/_MOC]] takes these requirements and turns them into architecture
- **Parallel:** [[04-domain-knowledge/_MOC]] provides the expert knowledge that makes the AI possible
- **Validation:** [[05-gap-analysis/_MOC]] checks these promises against reality
